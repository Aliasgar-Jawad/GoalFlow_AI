System Architecture

Engineered Against Human Failure Modes

GoalFlow is not another to-do list. It is an algorithmic framework that solves the psychological and logistical reasons people fail their goals.

Deterministic Capacity Guard

Prevents cognitive burnout before it starts. The mathematical validation engine guarantees planned weekly minutes will never exceed your confirmed budget.

Exam & Blackout Protection

Never schedules tasks during midterm exams, travel dates, or critical deadlines. Hard calendar constraints are strictly respected by the scheduling algorithms.

DAG Topological Sort

Guarantees zero circular dependencies. High-level tasks are broken down into a Directed Acyclic Graph so prerequisites are always completed in strict sequence.

Effort vs Outcome Separation

Solves the busywork illusion. Measures actual verified deliverables and key result evidence separately from hours logged, so you never confuse effort with mastery.
